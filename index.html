Currently at Qwen

I am an M.S. student in Artificial Intelligence at Tsinghua University. I received my B.Eng. in Artificial Intelligence from Dalian University of Technology.

My research interests lie in multimodal foundation models and agentic systems. I am particularly interested in models that can perceive complex environments, reason over long horizons, and take reliable actions through graphical interfaces, tools, and code.

I am currently a research intern with the Qwen Team. Previously, I worked with the Foundation Model Team at Huawei Multimodal Lab and conducted a remote research internship at Microsoft Research Asia.

I am open to research conversations and collaboration. More contact information, publications, and a full CV will be added after this private draft is finalized.
